Causes, against the CUAD category descriptions (data/testset/categories.tsv):
- Missing labels: in Q01m, Q06m/p and Q08p the fine-tuned model ranks first or near the top a clause that
  fits the category description but is not annotated in CUAD, so it counts as not relevant. With R = 1-2
  one missing label is enough to score 0.
- Relevance rule: in Q03p two chunks in the top 10 each hold most of a labelled span, but neither holds a
  whole span or has half its characters inside one, so neither is relevant. The span at
  HealthcentralCom 3618-3845 is split between chunks 693 and 694 and has no relevant chunk at all; over the
  whole test set 2 of 92 spans have none (one Covenant Not To Sue, one Non-Transferable License).
- Chunk packing: the Liquidated Damages chunks are relevant only by containing a whole short span; the
  labelled text is 10-20% of chunks of 865 and 1218 characters, so the rest of the chunk sets the ranking.
- Loose labels and wording: the Liquidated Damages spans never say "liquidated damages" (for example "This
  Agreement can be terminated at any time prior to the expiry of the Term, as follows:"), and Q01p says
  "companies in the same corporate group" where the contract says "Affiliates".
