Offline semantic search for legal documents

Find the clause,
not the keyword.

Ask in plain English. QorFinder reads the PDF, Word and text files in a folder and shows the passages that mean what you asked, even when they use different words. It runs on your own computer and sends nothing anywhere.

Built for a desk, not a data centre

Everything happens in one program on your computer: no account, no cloud and no setup.

Search by meaning

Type a question the way you would ask a colleague. A small language model compares its meaning with every passage, so a match does not hang on the exact words.

Stays on your computer

The model runs on your CPU. QorFinder has no network code at all; its page is served only to your own machine, behind a random key.

PDF, Word and text

Point it at a folder and it reads every PDF, DOCX and TXT file inside, subfolders too. Scanned or locked files are skipped, each with a reason.

Points to the place

Every result names the file, the page and the exact passage, and opens the file in its usual app with one click.

Unzip and run

One folder with the program, both models and the interface. Nothing to install, on Windows or Linux. MIT-licensed and open source.

Two models

The default is fine-tuned for contracts; a general English model suits other documents. Pick one when you index a folder.

How it works

Indexing does the slow part once, so every question after it is fast.

  1. 1

    Read

    Text comes out of each file page by page, and QorFinder remembers where every character came from.

  2. 2

    Split

    Documents are cut at paragraphs and numbered clauses (12.3, (a)) into passages of about 150 words.

  3. 3

    Embed

    The model turns each passage into 384 numbers that capture its meaning, and stores them in the index.

  4. 4

    Search

    Your question becomes 384 numbers too, and the passages closest to it come back first.

Get started in three steps

1Download

Get the zip for your system from the latest release and unzip it anywhere.

2Start it

Open a terminal in the unzipped folder and run:

Windows  .\qorfinder.exe serve
Linux    ./qorfinder serve

Your browser opens QorFinder.

3Pick a folder, then ask

Under Documents, choose the folder with your contracts and index it. Then ask your question under Search.

Prefer the command line? 
qorfinder index ~/contracts
qorfinder search "can the employee work for a competitor after leaving?"

Good to know

  • English only. Both models were trained on English text.
  • No scanned documents. A PDF needs a text layer; QorFinder does not read text from images.
  • Indexing takes a while. About 7 seconds per contract on a 2019 laptop; searching afterwards takes milliseconds.
  • Results are leads, not advice. The right clause is not always first, and now and then a heading on its own slips in. Always read the clause itself before you rely on it.
